About

Web App Developer & UI/UX Designer

Hello! I'm Emon Dhar, a passionate and creative IT professional with a strong interest in web application development, UI/UX design, mobile application development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and digital marketing.

I have completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science & Engineering from Daffodil International University. My academic background has helped me build a strong foundation in programming, software development, database management, problem solving, and modern technology.

I enjoy transforming ideas and real-world problems into practical digital solutions. My development approach focuses on creating applications that are responsive, user-friendly, functional, maintainable, and visually appealing.

As a Web App Developer, I work with technologies such as HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Python, SQLite, and modern web development concepts. I am also interested in building practical applications using Python and integrating AI and machine learning into digital products.

Alongside development, I have a strong interest in UI/UX Design. I enjoy designing clean interfaces, creating wireframes and prototypes, developing design systems, and improving user experiences through user-centered design principles.

I also have experience and interest in mobile application development, particularly Android applications. I focus on creating simple, reliable, and practical mobile experiences with clean interfaces and useful functionality.

Another area of my interest is Digital Marketing. I am interested in SEO, social media marketing, content strategy, and digital growth. I believe that successful digital products require not only good technology and design, but also an effective strategy for reaching the right audience.

My goal is to continuously improve my technical and creative skills and work on meaningful projects where technology, design, and business objectives come together.

I am always interested in learning new technologies, exploring innovative ideas, collaborating with people, and taking on challenging projects that allow me to grow professionally.
